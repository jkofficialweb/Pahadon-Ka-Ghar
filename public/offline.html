Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indian or foreign tourists require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to visit Hariganiwan or Sonamarg?

No permit or ILP is required for Indian citizens or foreign travelers to stay at Hotel Pahadon Ka Ghar or explore the Sindh Valley, Kangan, and Sonamarg. You only need a valid government-issued photo ID for standard hotel check-in.

What heating amenities are provided in the rooms during cold nights?

All suites are fitted with electric heated bed blankets, insulated double-glazed windows, thick thermal quilts, and 24/7 instant hot water geysers. Additional radiant electric heaters are activated during autumn and winter.
